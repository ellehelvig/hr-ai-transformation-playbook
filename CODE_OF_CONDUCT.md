# Code of conduct

This is a practitioner project. People contributing here work in HR, legal, people analytics, and engineering, often under real pressure to get AI programs right. Keep that in mind.

## Expectations

- Be direct and be kind. Critique the work, not the person.
- Cite sources for legal and regulatory claims. "I read somewhere" isn't a citation.
- Never contribute real employee or candidate data, even anonymized. Synthetic only.
- Disagree in the open. If you think a governance position is wrong, say so in an issue with your reasoning.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind
- Sharing anyone's private information
- Using this repo to promote a specific vendor without disclosing the relationship

## Enforcement

The maintainer will remove content or block contributors who don't follow this. Report concerns to ellehelvig@gmail.com.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, shortened to what actually matters here.
