I'm the HR lead at a 300-person distribution company, all US (Ohio and Texas). My CEO asked where HR is with AI and what we should do first. My notes: HR team is 4 people. We ran a 6-week pilot of an AI chatbot on our HR helpdesk for warehouse staff with a CSAT target, and it went okay. No AI policy. No list of tools. IT approves software but doesn't look at AI. CEO is excited but nobody owns HR AI decisions. Our ATS turned on an "AI match score" for applicants last spring and recruiters sort by it. HRIS data is messy and exports go out by email to whoever asks. No AI training for the team, a couple of us use a chat assistant for job descriptions. Never talked to legal about any of it. We're mid open enrollment and one person could give maybe 3 hours a week. Where are we on a maturity scale, and what do we do first?
