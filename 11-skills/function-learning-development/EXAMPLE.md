# Worked example: learning and development

Harbor & Pine is a fictional company. All data here is synthetic.

## Request

Marisol Fenn, L&D lead, writes: "We need AI literacy for all 1,400 staff, 210 of them in Germany, before the drafting assistant reaches all 310 support reps. Since the August pilot, reopened tickets rose from 41 to 58 per 1,000 closed. Build the plan and evaluation. Also pull the 20 reps with the highest pilot quiz scores from the LMS for our AI champion cohort."

## Output (abridged)

### Problem and owner

Gap: reps send AI-drafted replies without checking them against policy. Target: reopened tickets back to 41 or fewer per 1,000 closed. Owners: Marisol Fenn (design); Dev Halloran, VP Support (result).

### AI literacy plan

Tiers are cumulative: everyone takes tier 1.

| Tier | Audience and headcount | AI systems used | Topics | Record kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 All staff | 1,400 | Enterprise assistant | Policy, data exposure, inaccurate output, job security | LMS completion, internal record for Germany staff |
| 2 Regular users | 1,050 | Assistant, support drafting tool | Checking output against policy, prompt practice, shared prompt library | LMS completion |
| 3 Overseers | 160 | Hiring, performance, pay tools | Human review, bias, logging, notice | Completion plus signed review checklist |
| 4 Builders | 40 | Agent configuration | Testing, incident reporting | Completion |

Reps practice in peer groups on live tickets; team leads go first.

### Evaluation plan

| Level | Measure | Baseline | Comparison group | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 Behavior | Sampled AI-drafted replies with a policy check logged | Week 1 sample | Cohort B | Day 90 |
| 4 Results | Reopened tickets per 1,000 closed | 58 | Cohort B | Day 90 |

Cohort A (155 reps) starts in January; cohort B (155) starts in March and is the comparison until then. 155 + 155 = 310.

### Decision rights calls

- Drafted for sign-off: the literacy and evaluation plans (Marisol Fenn, with Legal for the legal module).
- Refused and routed: "pull the 20 reps with the highest quiz scores." Selecting named people is not the agent's call; quiz scores measure recall, not skill in the work. Routed to HR with draft criteria: tier 2 complete, two hours a week of protected time confirmed by the manager, open self-nomination, and an aggregate access check before HR chooses.

### Audit trail

- Inputs used: headcounts, pilot ticket figures. `[GAP: no taxonomy level for "policy judgment"]`.
- Tools called and fields read: HRIS export (job family, location, aggregate counts); LMS (quiz results by cohort only).
- Items needing sign-off: both plans, champion criteria.
- Open legal questions: EU AI Act, AI literacy (Article 4) for Germany staff; EU AI Act, transparency if an AI tutor is added. See the Key dates rows; counsel confirms.

### Metrics the plan will track

Behavior transfer rate, development access ratio by group, AI literacy record coverage.

### Next step

Marisol Fenn approves; HR owns champion selection.
