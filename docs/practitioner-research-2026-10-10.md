## Three improvements, one journey

| Improvement | HR problem and role relevance | Smallest useful artifact | Maintenance burden |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Use-case decision record](../01-use-cases/prioritization-matrix.md) | Attractive ideas can outrun evidence. Shows transformation prioritization and People systems readiness | Existing matrix with non-compensable gates, ownership, adoption, and a completed fictional hold decision | Review at scope/tool/data changes; revisit priorities quarterly |
| [People Partner redesign](../01-use-cases/work-redesign-people-partner.md) | Correct topic labels can miss sensitive context. Shows task design, HR judgment, and accountable handoffs | Short before/after narrative above the preserved detailed task map | Review after a policy, workflow, or evaluation change |
| [Pilot and adoption plan](../04-enablement/adoption-playbook.md) | Training and time estimates can be mistaken for value. Shows sponsorship, manager enablement, and benefit evaluation | Existing adoption page with a synthetic-first plan, measurement cadence, and decision gates | Weekly during pilot; retain version and owner at each decision |

NIST informs the risk-management discipline; Anthropic motivates task-level examination; Microsoft motivates organizational and manager routines. Workday informs the lifecycle-owner question without adding an integration. None supplies the example's thresholds or validates its outcomes.
