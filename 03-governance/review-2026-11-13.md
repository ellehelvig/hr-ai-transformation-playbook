## Maintenance

Review passed dates monthly and material legal changes when identified. Before adoption, assign an HR owner and qualified counsel to verify jurisdiction, coverage, operative provisions, exceptions, rulemaking, and court orders. Record the exact section, source version, checked date, and remaining uncertainty. This is a maintenance instruction, not a scheduled automation.
